Hi! I am a Research Fellow at Microsoft India, working with the PROSE team. I graduated with a B.Tech in Mathematics and Computing from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru.

My research interests are broadly differential privacy and privacy-preserving machine learning. My current projects focus on differentially private regression and optimization, private fine-tuning of large language models.

I have also interned at Flipkart (one of India's leading e-commerce companies) and PrivaSapien (a privacy engineering and responsible AI startup), and collaborated with Prof. Lalitha Sankar and Prof. Oliver Kosut at Arizona State University, Prof. Rahul De at IIM Bangalore, and Prof. Sayak Ray Choudhury at IIT Kanpur.

Please feel free to take a look at my CV.
